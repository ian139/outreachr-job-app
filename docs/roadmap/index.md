## Reading rules

- **Current fact** means directly observed in this worktree's source/contracts; it does not mean the behavior was exercised.
- **Proposed** means a contract or implementation plan for the fork. Proposed items require the acceptance gates in the roadmap.
- “Dormant” means a page/component file exists in `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/pages/`, while `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/App.tsx` currently routes only `/applications`, `/inbox`, and `/settings/*`.
- No secrets, credentials, private email content, or copied seed records belong in roadmap artifacts.
